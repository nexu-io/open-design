# High-Impact Promotion Poster

Create one flat promotional image. The offer and product must be understood in under three seconds without turning the design into a price-tag collage.

## Inputs

- product and immutable packaging or appearance details
- exact offer, timing, product name, and optional CTA
- channel, crop, logo safe area, and legal constraints
- brand palette and desired level of visual intensity

Never invent discounts, prices, availability, endorsements, guarantees, QR codes, logos, or legal copy.

## Art direction

1. Rank the message: offer first, product second, urgency third, supporting mark last.
2. Choose one physical action that unites product and type: tear through, stack, stamp, peel, crush, splash, or slice.
3. Keep the product recognizable and physically supported; match count, packaging, labels, and materials exactly.
4. Use a bright field, oversized typography, and two or three saturated accents with open shadows.
5. Add credible retail-production texture such as torn paper, condensation, print grain, adhesive edge, or stamped ink.
6. Reserve quiet space around legal or editable copy when requested.

Use the host image-generation capability and save one finished bitmap.

## Reject generic AI styling

No glossy black pedestal, floating product, generic burst rays, fake pricing, extra packaging, invented logo, random badges, neon cyberpunk, excessive bloom, liquid-metal 3D type, filler microcopy, malformed product controls, or presentation mockup.

## Quality gate

- The exact offer is the first readable message.
- Product count, shape, label, and material remain correct.
- The physical hook explains the composition instead of merely decorating it.
- Color is bright and commercial without losing hierarchy.
- No accidental claim, fake brand element, or unreadable text appears.

## Demo brief

Create a 4:5 beverage promotion poster using only **DOUBLE UP**, **BUY 1 GET 1**, **FRIDAY ONLY**, and **FIZZ/01**. Show exactly two slim silver cans with acid-lime labels punching through tangerine torn-paper letterforms on pale aqua.
