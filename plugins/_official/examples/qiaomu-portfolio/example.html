About

The brief is mine.
The busywork is the agent's.

I went independent in 2019, not to escape teams but to work closer to the decision. No middle layer between me and the client means faster calls and cleaner execution.

Most product problems are strategy problems wearing a design costume, so my work starts with defining what to solve. For the production itself I direct OpenDesign agents: they draft the prototypes, decks, and imagery on my own API keys, and I edit until it is right.
