Executive recommendation

We recommend a four-phase pilot that aligns the partner and Open Design on the highest-value document workflows, turns those workflows into usable templates, and leaves the team with a review-ready system for continued iteration.

Roles & cadence

A small decision group keeps the pilot moving.

  • Partner sponsorSets priorities, resolves trade-offs and approves each phase outcome.
  • Contributor groupSupplies source material and joins one focused review each week.
  • Open DesignFacilitates working sessions, produces the template system and records decisions.
Commercial assumptions

Scope stays transparent as the pilot develops.

Timing, commercial terms and any additional workflow requests are confirmed in the statement of work. The pilot assumes timely source access, one named sponsor and consolidated feedback from the partner team.
