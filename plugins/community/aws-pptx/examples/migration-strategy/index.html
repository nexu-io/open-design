Strategy

The 6 R's

One strategy per workload, not one strategy for everything

  • Rehost — fastest, lowest risk; 80% of estate goes here first
  • Replatform — small surgery for big upside (Oracle → Aurora)
  • Refactor — only when the runway and value justify it
  • Repurchase — replace with SaaS where commodity (CRM, email)
  • Retire — kill the unloved; surveys reveal ~15% has no users
  • Retain — leave on-prem when latency or compliance demands it

Governance

Guardrails, not gates

  • Preventive — SCPs deny region pinning, root user keys, public S3 from creation
  • Detective — Config rules score every account against CIS + custom benchmarks daily
  • Responsive — EventBridge auto-remediates 14 of the top 20 misconfig patterns
  • Audit — CloudTrail Lake is the source of truth for SOC 2 evidence collection
  • Tagging — mandatory cost-center, env, owner via SCP

FinOps

Cost as a first-class concern from day one

  • Tag everything — SCP rejects untagged resource creation in prod
  • Show, don't scold — every team gets a QuickSight cost dashboard scoped to their tag
  • Buy savings, not surprises — Compute Savings Plans cover the steady-state 70%
  • Anomaly Detection — pages on-call when daily spend deviates > 25% from baseline
  • Quarterly forecast — finance + engineering review forecast vs actual together

Summary

Three things to take back to the team

  1. Lift the 80% first Rehost the workloads that won't change in twelve months — keep the muscle for the parts that will.
  2. Land before any wave Multi-account Landing Zone, Transit Gateway, and SCPs in place before workload one moves.
  3. Cost from day one Tag everything, set per-team budgets, and review forecast vs actual every quarter.
