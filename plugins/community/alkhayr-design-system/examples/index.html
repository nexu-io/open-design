Alkhayr Waqf Charity Foundation — Design System

Tokens, components, and page patterns for the six-page site — Home, About Us, Our Waqf Vision, Projects, Donate, Contact. Built from the two real assets that exist: the logo (colors pixel-sampled, shapes inherited) and the drafted copy (voice). One idea governs everything: permanence — give once, and the asset keeps giving.

Ilorin, Nigeria6 pagesBricolage Grotesque · one family 2 greens · gold in reservelight + dark tokens

/01 · Foundation

Positioning

Alkhayr is not running a one-off appeal — it builds income-generating waqf assets (first: a student hostel in Ilorin) whose profits fund orphan care, education, healthcare, entrepreneurship, and poverty relief for as long as the asset stands. Every visual and verbal choice should feel like an institution that plans to exist in fifty years, not a campaign that ends next month. That means: quiet confidence over urgency, documentation over decoration, specifics over sentiment.

/02 · Tokens

Color

Three brand colors, each with a job, all sampled directly from the logo. The logo itself splits its greens: near-black deep green carries the ink; the brighter sprig green appears only on the leaf — so sprig becomes the action color, tying every "donate" moment to the mark's growth symbol. Gold is the mark's only warm color and stays in reserve. Ratios measured, not estimated.

/03 · Tokens

Typography

One family — Bricolage Grotesque — across the whole system. Weight, not family, carries the hierarchy: extra-bold (800) for trust moments (headlines, the Hadith, the tagline), bold (700) for headings and labels, regular (400) for everything operational — donation steps, bank details, forms. 800 never below 29px. The family ships no italics — quotes stay upright at 500. Mono is for figures and references only.

Rules: ALL CAPS always tracked ≥ +.10em. Display sizes always negative-tracked. Weight ladder: 800 display, 700 headings, 400 body — 500 upright for quotes (the family has no italic axis; never synthesize one). Body measure ≤ 65ch. Never recreate the logo's calligraphy as a text style — it is a logotype. Load real Arabic (Amiri) if the site ever carries Arabic copy.

/15 · Usage notes

Voice, imagery, devices, accessibility

The rules that keep the system coherent after it leaves this page.

Voice — from the drafted copy, not invented

On brand

"Revenue from the hostel will support orphan care, educational scholarships, healthcare support, and community welfare programs."

Off brand

"Your generous gift transforms lives and brings hope to those who need it most! 🙏"

On brand

"Waqf is a permanent charitable endowment in Islam where an asset is dedicated for the benefit of society."

Off brand

"Waqf (an ancient Islamic tradition passed down through the ages) is our unique way of giving back…"

Graphic devices — inherited from the mark

Mihrab archImage masks, section dividers, the quote-band glyph. "A place built to last." Never a container shape.
CrescentSmall moments only — a bullet, a favicon, a loading state. Never large enough to compete with the arch.
Hands & sprig"Your donation, growing" — impact stats, funding progress, the donate hero. The model made visible.

Imagery

  • a

    Document the real hostel — groundbreaking, construction, the finished building, its residents. Plain, honest photography as it happens.

  • b

    Never NGO stock — no posed "distressed child" shots, no unrelated stock mosques. Credibility comes from the actual project.

  • c

    Honest placeholders until then — a render, a site plan, a labelled slot (as used throughout this document).

Hard rules

  • 1

    Sprig is the only action color, at most twice per screen. Gold is reserve: arch outlines, eyebrows on dark, rare badges — never button fills, never adjacent to sprig.

  • 2

    Weight carries the split, not family. Headlines and the Hadith at 800; donation steps, bank details, and forms at 400/600. Bricolage Grotesque everywhere.

  • 3

    No invented numbers. No "₦ raised" counters, no percentage goals, no impact stats the foundation can't evidence. Honest placeholders instead.

  • 4

    One dark section per page. Ground Deep is ceremony — the quote band, the footer, the bank card. It loses meaning if everything is dark.

  • 5

    Faith-grounded without over-explaining. Use Waqf, Sadaqah Jariyah, and the Hadith naturally, trusting the reader — as the drafted copy already does.
