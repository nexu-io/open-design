# XIDIMA
## Complete Brand Identity System

A Tunisian marketplace platform connecting people with trusted professionals and service providers, launching from Sfax.

---

## 1. BRAND STRATEGY

### Brand Mission
To bridge the gap between skilled professionals and people who need their services, making quality service access simple, transparent, and trustworthy across Tunisia.

### Brand Vision
Become Tunisia's most trusted marketplace for local professional services, creating economic opportunity for skilled workers while delivering exceptional value to customers.

### Brand Values
- **Trustworthiness**: Verified professionals, transparent ratings, secure transactions
- **Quality**: Premium service standards and professional excellence
- **Accessibility**: Easy to use, inclusive platform for all service categories
- **Community**: Building strong local connections within Sfax and beyond
- **Innovation**: Modern technology serving traditional craftsmanship
- **Transparency**: Clear pricing, honest reviews, fair practices

### Brand Promise
"Every Service Meets Its Craftsman" — We connect you with the right professional for your needs, ensuring quality, trust, and reliability every time.

### Brand Positioning Statement
XIDIMA is a premium, modern marketplace for professional services in Tunisia that combines trusted craftspeople with technology-enabled convenience, positioning itself as the essential service connection platform.

### Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
**Verified Local Professionals + Transparent Marketplace + Modern Technology + Tunisian Heritage**

We're not just another freelancer app. We're a trusted marketplace that honors the craftsmanship tradition while providing modern convenience, connecting you directly with verified professionals who are invested in your community.

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Arabic Font (Optimal for Regional Appeal)

**Primary Arabic Font: Cairo or Noto Kufi Arabic**
- Modern, geometric aesthetics
- Perfect for Tunisia's sophisticated market
- Excellent readability on screens and print
- Alternative: Traditional Droid Arabic Kufi for heritage feel

**Usage:**
- All Arabic content (primary market language)
- Headlines in Arabic sections
- Body text for Arabic speakers
- Culturally appropriate and professional

### French Font

**Primary: Graphik or Inter**
- Clean geometric sans-serif
- Colonial history connection (French influence)
- Professional marketplace standard
- Excellent readability

**Alternative: Montserrat**
- Urban, modern aesthetic
- Strong geometric structure
- Widely available web font

### English Font

**Primary: Inter or Space Grotesk**
- Modern, international standard
- Clear, accessible typography
- Tech-forward aesthetic
- Excellent for B2B communication

### Heading Font
**Primary: Poppins Bold or Rubik Bold**
- Strong, distinctive personality
- Modern but approachable
- Works across all three languages
- Excellent for visual hierarchy

### Body Font
**Primary: Inter or Roboto Regular**
- Optimized for screen readability
- Professional and clean
- Consistent across platforms
- 16px minimum for accessibility

### UI Font (Navigation/UI Elements)
**Primary: Inter Medium**
- Slightly heavier than body
- Better for buttons and navigation
- Maintains hierarchy
- 14px-15px for optimal readability

### Font Usage Hierarchy

#### Web & Digital
```
Heading (H1): Poppins Bold, 48px, Navy Blue (#0E1F3A)
Subheading (H2): Poppins SemiBold, 36px, Navy Blue
Section Title (H3): Poppins Medium, 28px, Navy Blue
Subsection (H4): Inter Bold, 20px, Dark Charcoal
Body Text: Inter Regular, 16px, Dark Charcoal
Secondary Text: Inter Regular, 14px, Medium Gray
Caption: Inter Regular, 12px, Medium Gray
Button Text: Inter Medium, 14px-16px, White or Navy
```

#### Print & Marketing
```
Headlines: Cairo or Graphik Bold, 32-48pt
Subheadings: Cairo or Graphik SemiBold, 24pt
Body: Inter or similar, 11pt
Captions: Inter, 9pt
```

#### Application Hierarchy
1. **Primary Action**: Heading font, larger size, navy or orange
2. **Secondary Action**: Subheading font, medium size, navy
3. **Supporting Information**: Body font, dark charcoal
4. **De-emphasized**: Secondary text color, smaller size

### Typographic Rules
- **Line Height**: 1.6 for body text (16px), 1.4 for headings
- **Letter Spacing**: +0.5px for headings, 0 for body
- **Font Weight**: Limit to 3-4 weights maximum (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold)
- **Multilingual Consistency**: Arabic fonts maintain same x-height and visual weight as Latin fonts

## 5. DESIGN LANGUAGE

### Design System Specifications

#### Border Radius
- **Sharp Elements** (Professional/Minimal): 0px (buttons, major sections)
- **Subtle Rounding** (Modern/Friendly): 4px (input fields, small components)
- **Rounded** (Accent/Card-like): 8px (cards, modal backgrounds)
- **Fully Rounded** (Special): 50% or 24px+ (profile avatars, badges)

#### Shadow System
```
Elevation 1 (Subtle): 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)
Elevation 2 (Cards): 0 4px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)
Elevation 3 (Modals): 0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)
Elevation 4 (Dropdowns): 0 8px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)
Elevation 5 (Floating): 0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.20)
```

#### Icon Style
- **Style**: Minimalist, geometric, line-based
- **Stroke Width**: 2px for consistency
- **Size Grid**: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px (multiples of 8)
- **Philosophy**: Clear, recognizable, professional
- **Color**: Navy primary, orange for important actions
- **Examples**: 
  - Location pin (place icon)
  - Star (ratings)
  - Checkmark (verification)
  - User profile (professional headshots)

#### Card Style
- **Base**: White background on light backgrounds, #1A2F4E on dark backgrounds
- **Border**: None or 1px #E0E0E0 (light) / 1px #2C3E50 (dark)
- **Padding**: 24px horizontal, 20px vertical
- **Shadow**: Elevation 2
- **Hover**: 2% darker background, Elevation 3
- **Corner Radius**: 8px

#### Grid System
- **Column Grid**: 12-column responsive
- **Gutters**: 
  - Desktop (1200px+): 24px
  - Tablet (768px-1199px): 16px
  - Mobile (< 768px): 12px
- **Breakpoints**: 320px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px
- **Container Max Width**: 1400px

#### Layout Principles
1. **Predictable Spacing**: All spacing in multiples of 8px (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64)
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Larger, bolder, warmer = more important
3. **Alignment**: Left-align text, center primary CTAs
4. **Density**: Professional but not cramped (adequate whitespace)
5. **Asymmetry**: Use strategic imbalance for visual interest
6. **Readability First**: Never sacrifice clarity for aesthetics

#### White Space Rules
- **Micro-spacing**: 8px (between form elements)
- **Small-spacing**: 16px (between sections within containers)
- **Medium-spacing**: 24px (between major sections)
- **Large-spacing**: 48px (between page sections)

## 6. ILLUSTRATION STYLE

### Design Philosophy
Combine Tunisian geometric heritage with modern, clean vector illustration. Illustrations should feel fresh, contemporary, yet distinctly Tunisian—never generic.

### Key Characteristics
- **Geometry-Based**: Use circles, squares, triangles, hexagons
- **Tunisian Zellige Inspiration**: Tile patterns, tessellations, octagons
- **Minimalist Approach**: Reductive, simplified forms
- **Flat Design**: No gradients, 3D effects, or shadows
- **Clean Outlines**: Consistent 2-3px stroke weights

### Shape Language
- **Primary Shapes**:
  - Octagons (Tunisian zellige reference)
  - Circles (unity, community)
  - Hexagons (connectivity, structure)
  - Triangles (direction, growth)
  - Squares (stability, trustworthiness)

- **Composition Rules**:
  - Build from geometric foundations
  - Maintain 60% geometric, 40% organic curve
  - Use shape nesting and overlap for depth
  - Ensure proportional consistency

### Pattern Library
- **Geometric Tile Patterns**: Octagons with internal star patterns (zellige)
- **Border Patterns**: Interlocking shapes as framing devices
- **Background Patterns**: Subtle tile repetition at 5-15% opacity
- **Corner Ornaments**: Decorative geometric flourishes for key sections

### Color in Illustrations
- **Primary Colors**: Navy and orange (brand colors)
- **Extended Palette**: Add 2-3 supporting colors per illustration context
- **Light Mode**: Use full navy, full orange, supporting colors
- **Dark Mode**: Use lighter tints, full orange, muted supporting colors
- **Contrast Rule**: Maintain minimum 4:5:1 contrast ratio

### Line Styles
- **Stroke Weight**: Consistent 2px for main illustrations
- **Stroke Caps**: Round caps for organic feel
- **Stroke Joins**: Round joins for softness
- **Dashed Lines**: 4px dash + 4px gap for subtle division

### Illustration Categories

#### 1. Professional Services Icons
- Electrician (lightbulb + wrench design)
- Plumber (pipe + wrench design)
- Painter (brush + paint can)
- Carpenter (saw + wood)
- Mason (brick + level)
- Gardener (plant + shovel)
- **Style**: Geometric, minimal, instantly recognizable
- **Usage**: Landing page categories, service selection

#### 2. Lifestyle/Situational Illustrations
- Professional at work
- Customer receiving service
- Team collaboration
- Problem-solving moment
- **Style**: Simplified human figures with geometric shapes
- **Usage**: Value proposition sections, "How it works" flows

#### 3. Empty States & Feedback
- Empty search results (magnifying glass + geometric shapes)
- Error state (warning symbol, clean design)
- Success completion (checkmark surrounded by geometric celebration)
- Loading state (animated circles in geometric pattern)
- **Style**: Minimal, calming, supportive
- **Usage**: Application UI feedback

#### 4. Feature Illustrations
- Trust/verification (shield + checkmark + geometric elements)
- Community (multiple circles connecting)
- Transparency (open hands with information)
- Speed/efficiency (geometric lines suggesting movement)
- **Style**: Abstract, conceptual, modern
- **Usage**: Feature highlight sections

#### 5. Background Decorative Elements
- Corner flourishes (geometric patterns)
- Section dividers (tessellating shapes)
- Texture overlays (subtle tile patterns)
- Accent borders (interlocking geometric lines)
- **Style**: 3-8% opacity, never competing with content
- **Usage**: Background layers, section separation

### Architectural Elements
- **Sfax landmarks**: Simplified geometric interpretations
- **Buildings**: Clean silhouettes with geometric divisions
- **Storefronts**: Minimalist shop representations
- **Craftspeople**: Geometric human figures with tools
- **Usage**: Regional identity, sense of place

### Animation Guidance
- **Geometric shapes**: Can morph and transform smoothly
- **Stroke animations**: Lines can draw in progressively
- **Color transitions**: Smooth shifts between navy/orange
- **Entrance effects**: Shapes appear with slight scale and fade
- **Loop duration**: 2-4 seconds for ambient animations

## 7. PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE

### Core Aesthetic
Authentic, professional, diverse, well-lit. Show real people, real work, real impact. No stock-photo feeling—genuine Tunisian professionals and customers.

### Worker/Professional Photography

#### Photography Guidelines
- **Lighting**: Natural light preferred, soft and diffused
- **Background**: Minimal, blurred, or subtle work environment
- **Framing**: Headshots (shoulders up) or action shots
- **Eyes**: Direct eye contact, friendly and confident
- **Styling**: Professional but relatable (work clothes okay, overly formal is false)
- **Diversity**: Represent all genders, ages, backgrounds
- **Authenticity**: Real expressions, not forced smiles

#### Composition Rules
- **Headshots**: Center face in frame, head-to-shoulder framing
- **Action Shots**: Show skill in action (electrician working, etc.)
- **Environmental Shots**: Professional in their element with tools/space
- **Close-ups**: Hands at work (craft detail)
- **Group Shots**: Team dynamics, collaboration

#### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1080px width for digital
- **Aspect Ratios**: 1:1 (profiles), 3:2 (featured), 16:9 (hero)
- **Color Profile**: sRGB for web, converted to #0E1F3A tone
- **Retouching**: Light touch—skin smoothing but real texture maintained
- **Consistency**: Similar lighting and color temperature across gallery

### Freelancer Photography

#### Style Requirements
- **Professional but approachable**: This is their livelihood
- **Diverse professions**: Not just tech—include all service types
- **Genuine moments**: Captured naturally, not staged
- **Workspace**: Show their actual setup when possible
- **Tools of trade**: Include relevant equipment in frame
- **Personality**: Let their character shine through

#### Composition Approach
- **Hero shot**: One professional per series with prominent placement
- **Growth focus**: Show progression, skill, journey
- **Customer testimonials**: Photo + quote combination
- **Success stories**: Before/after scenarios

### Team Photography

#### Guidelines
- **Cohesion**: Visual consistency in lighting and color treatment
- **Professionalism**: Business casual minimum
- **Authenticity**: Real team moments, not rigid lineups
- **Diversity**: Reflect Tunisian diversity in team representation
- **Energy**: Dynamic poses, genuine interactions

#### Situations to Document
- Team meetings and collaboration
- Individual contributor portraits
- Company values in action
- Customer service moments
- Company culture/behind-the-scenes

### Marketing/Campaign Imagery

#### Scenarios to Feature
1. **Customer Success**
   - Happy customer with completed service
   - Before/after transformations
   - Testimonial moments

2. **Professional Excellence**
   - Skilled hands at work
   - Quality details
   - Problem-solving in action

3. **Community Building**
   - Local pride (Sfax landmarks in background)
   - Team celebrations
   - Customer appreciation

4. **Lifestyle Integration**
   - Professional service improving daily life
   - Convenience/accessibility
   - Trust in action

#### Technical Treatment
- **Color Grading**: Warm tones, slightly desaturated, navy tint overlay optional
- **Cropping**: Tight on subjects, minimal negative space
- **Filters**: None, or very subtle warmth increase
- **Saturation**: 85-95% (not oversaturated)
- **Contrast**: Natural to slightly increased

### Photo Sourcing Recommendations
1. **Primary**: Commission original photography (best for authenticity)
2. **Secondary**: Curated premium stock with heavy customization
3. **Avoid**: Generic freelancer stock photos, unrealistic scenarios, obvious staging
