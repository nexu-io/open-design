# AI Image Prompt

## Main Prompt

Create an original premium advertising poster for **OnlyHub ($HUB)**, a Solana memecoin.

Creative concept: **“The internet keeps loading. The night keeps going.”**

Design a deep near-black neon nightclub environment. In the center, place a futuristic digital loading interface that transforms into a glowing nightclub horizon. The loading bar should feel like a portal into the night: hot pink light flowing into electric purple, with one controlled orange accent.

Make **$HUB** the dominant, instantly readable focal point. Surround it with sophisticated editorial typography and subtle technical interface details. Use confident modern display typography, restrained monospace microcopy, cinematic bloom, soft neon haze, atmospheric depth, subtle film grain, and precise high-end art direction.

Composition: vertical 4:5 poster, strong centered hierarchy, generous negative space, balanced asymmetry, designed as a standalone social image.

Visual palette: hot pink, purple, deep near-black, one orange accent, off-white typography.

Mood: underground internet culture, after-hours digital nightlife, premium fashion campaign meets futuristic UI, playful but polished.

Text hierarchy:
1. ONLYHUB
2. $HUB — very large and unmistakably legible
3. THE INTERNET KEEPS LOADING.
4. THE NIGHT KEEPS GOING.
5. THE ONLY HUB THAT NEVER FINISHES LOADING.

Important constraints: original composition, no rockets, no laser eyes, no generic coin stacks, no charts, no overused crypto symbols, no clutter, no template appearance, no copied reference layout, no unreadable typography.

The final result should look like it was art-directed and paid for by a premium creative studio: sharp hierarchy, intentional spacing, clean typography, cinematic neon lighting, highly polished advertising design.
