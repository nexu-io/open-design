The starting point

A sales brochure has to survive the next revision.

In Japanese real estate, a sales brochure rarely belongs to one person, one channel, or one moment. The same property may move through different languages, agencies, and customer groups. A partner needs another logo. A salesperson needs a new phone number or URL. A Chinese or English version changes the words, hierarchy, spacing, and amount of information.

CATMAN needed the finished page to remain editable so his team could revise it, share source files, adapt it for another channel, and keep using it in the sales process.

CATMAN’s feedback tied OpenDesign to the work his team does every day.
What CATMAN’s practical feedback brought to OpenDesign
How he found OpenDesign

He began with the constraints of real-estate work.

CATMAN first connected with the team through Discord. He described the materials his work depended on: multilingual brochures, presentations, websites, source-file sharing, and repeated changes for different sales channels.

He could translate the realities of Japanese business workflows into concrete product questions about editability, reusable layouts, consistent visual systems, and practical file handoff. The ambassador relationship grew from those questions.

How it showed up · 01

A brochure became a working OpenDesign use case.

CATMAN and his team used OpenDesign to create brochures for investment properties in Osaka. The work combined structured property information, imagery, visual hierarchy, persuasive presentation, and the sense of trust a high-consideration purchase requires.

The finished brochure remained editable. The team could keep refining it for the next partner, language, or sales channel.

How it showed up · 02

He brought the workflow into the room.

At the OpenDesign Osaka meetup, CATMAN shared the brochure with designers, developers, educators, founders, and community organizers. The example worked because everyone could see the job the artifact needed to do: organize dense information, make a property feel credible, support different audiences, and remain editable when the next request arrived.
