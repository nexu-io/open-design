The starting point

Local-agent setup can push new users away before they make anything.

Josh focuses on non-technical creators and small businesses that need better design. They have little reason to spend their first session learning infrastructure.

They may already know how to describe an idea to a model and may need a brand deck, landing page, or social content. Local agents, API keys, model capabilities, and command-line setup can bury that useful creative task under unfamiliar choices. Josh helps people reach a useful result first, then explains the workflow behind it.

One missing dependency can end a first session before the user sees a result.
The kind of problem Josh handles in the community
How he found OpenDesign

Open Design Cloud Agent made the ambassador role feel concrete.

Josh joined through the OpenDesign Ambassador application while the team was introducing Open Design Cloud Agent, a simpler entry point to local-agent workflows.

His proposal focused on workshops for small and medium-sized businesses: begin with a branding deck, then carry the same direction into a landing page and social content. He wanted participants to leave with a useful result and enough understanding to repeat the process. That focus on first-time users led to his invitation into the ambassador community.

How it showed up · 01

His first contribution was helping someone get unstuck.

In OpenDesign’s Discord, Josh began answering practical questions. His short, specific replies moved workflows forward.

How it showed up · 02

He tested the path he wanted other people to trust.

Josh connected his own Reasonix local-agent instance to OpenDesign. When the reasoning view did not behave as expected, he raised the question, tested the setup, restarted the environment, and reported when it began working again.

Josh treats troubleshooting as product work. Each rough edge he encounters may stop a newcomer later. By testing in public, he gives the team and community a clearer view of the onboarding experience.

Documentation can describe the happy path. A person in the community can notice where someone has fallen off it and help them return.
