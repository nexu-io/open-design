Core capabilities

Design work, delegated

Every feature serves one goal: you describe the outcome, agents handle the production, and you get files you can actually ship.

Prototypes, slides, and images

One prompt can become a clickable prototype, a full slide deck, or launch imagery. Agents choose the right format and deliver finished artifacts, not mockups.

Templates worth remixing

Hundreds of decks, landing pages, and frame styles, each one a working file. Agents adapt any of them to your brand in minutes.

Design systems that stick

Point agents at your design system and every artifact comes back on brand. Colors, type, and spacing are enforced, not suggested.

Iterate in plain language

Not quite right? Say so. "Tighter hero copy, warmer palette, bigger CTA" is a valid design instruction, and the agent revises the file in place.
