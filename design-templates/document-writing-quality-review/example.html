Priority revisions

PriorityEditorial findingRecommended change
BLOCKEROpening sentence

“Works for every document workflow” is unsupported.

The current examples cover five directions, not every possible document task. The claim makes the launch note less credible.

Name the current scope.

Describe the five prepared directions and invite reviewers to identify gaps or unclear scenarios.

IMPORTANTSecond sentence

“Makes document creation effortless” is a vague promise.

The note does not identify what users can inspect or improve in the current template set.

Point to reviewable artifacts.

Say that each direction includes a structured example and a shareable HTML preview.

IMPORTANTClosing sentence

The call to action lacks a review task.

“Take a look” does not tell contributors what feedback would be useful.

Ask for a concrete check.

Invite reviewers to check scenario fit, first-screen priority and the clarity of the final document output.
