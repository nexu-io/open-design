FAQ

The questions your team will ask

How long does setup actually take?

Median time from signup to first generated report is 41 minutes. The only human step is approving the metric definitions Briefing proposes.

Where does our data live?

Connections are read-only and queries run against your systems; Briefing stores computed metrics and reports, never raw customer records. SOC 2 Type II report available on request.

Can we edit the drafted narrative?

Always. The draft is a starting point in your tone of voice. Most teams change one or two sentences; every edit teaches the next draft.

What if our metrics are messy?

That is the normal starting state. The one-time metric review surfaces every conflicting definition, and teams tell us that meeting alone was worth the trial.

What happens after the trial?

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes, in one click from settings, effective immediately, with a prorated refund on annual plans. No retention phone call.
