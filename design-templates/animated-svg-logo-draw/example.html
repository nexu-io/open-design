Animate the mark, not the whole page.

This fixture isolates one durable motion pattern: a masked stroke that draws through an original monogram, resolves crisply, and can replay without turning the surrounding UI into a tech demo.

  • Mask, then draw Keep the visible filled shape stable and animate a thicker stroke inside a mask when you want a drawn line with variable-width character.
  • Reduced-motion safe The mark should still read as a complete logo when motion is disabled or never triggered.
  • One memorable beat Use this for headers, splash states, or portfolio intros. Don't loop a signature mark across an entire site chrome.
