# Delivery checklist

## P0 — required

- The output is runnable HTML adapted from the real seed, not a screenshot.
- The brand, headline, services and FAQ reflect the supplied brief. No fabricated metrics, clients or endorsements.
- All images resolve offline; no unlicensed fonts or inspiration-site files are introduced.
- Menu, chapter anchors, accordions and contact dialog work with keyboard and pointer.
- Required fields validate. Saving is explicitly local-only unless a real backend was requested and connected.
- Dialog close and Escape restore focus to a visible trigger.
- At 390px and desktop widths, headings remain legible and document width does not exceed viewport width.
- Reduced-motion users see all content without parallax or continuous animation. Canvas pauses while hidden or offscreen.
- Preserve the LICENSE and ASSET-SOURCES.md provenance when redistributing the original seed.
