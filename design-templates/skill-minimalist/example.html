Questions, answered plainly

Where does my library live?

On your device first, synced end-to-end encrypted. Arbor works fully offline; a flight is a feature, not an outage.

Can I import from Zotero or Mendeley?

Yes. Drop in a BibTeX or RIS export and Arbor rebuilds your collections, PDFs, and annotations, usually in under a minute.

Does it work for co-authored papers?

Each author writes in their own copy; Arbor merges paragraph by paragraph and keeps a signed history of who changed which claim.

What happens if I cancel?

Your documents export to plain Markdown and BibTeX with one click, and remain readable locally forever. No hostage formats.
