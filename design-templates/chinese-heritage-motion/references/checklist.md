# Chinese Heritage Motion delivery checklist

## P0 gates

- [ ] The output is a standalone HTML page with all eight canonical scene families represented.
- [ ] Historical dates, dimensions, conservation claims, and structural facts are documented or marked interpretive/unverified before they appear in rendered copy.
- [ ] Every required asset resolves from the output-relative asset path; missing files fail composition instead of silently producing broken scenes.
- [ ] Scene families that share a camera derive from one master scene and do not imply unsupported exploded construction.
- [ ] The page remains readable without WebGL, with reduced motion, and at mobile widths; no horizontal overflow is introduced by scene transitions.
- [ ] Keyboard focus, image alternatives, and navigation controls remain usable through the full chapter sequence.
- [ ] The final page is checked at 360px, 768px, and 1440px before handoff.

## Content and quality

- [ ] The visual system stays materially specific to the heritage subject and does not drift into generic travel or SaaS language.
- [ ] Documentary imagery includes source/licensing metadata; generated imagery is labelled as visual interpretation rather than evidence.
- [ ] Motion follows the named scene preset and preserves the dense/quiet rhythm described in the template references.
