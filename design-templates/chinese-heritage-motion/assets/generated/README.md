# Generated example masters

Generated individually through the imagegen interface on 2026-09-22.
All three are 1536×1024 PNG images. These are artistic interpretations, not
photographs or measured representations of Yingxian Wooden Pagoda.

- `pagoda.png`: right-weighted ancient timber pagoda, blue-green sky, quiet left
  negative space, warm raking light, photographic material detail. Reused by
  encounter, verticality, preservation and afterimage.
- `timber.png`: close study of weathered interlocking timber brackets, repeated
  diagonals, warm light and dark recesses. Reused by structure, detail and time;
  overview/detail comparison is of the same image, not historical before/after.
- `interior.png`: dark timber columns and doorway looking toward a light mountain
  landscape; an independent atmospheric interior, not the verified pagoda interior.

No text, UI, fake dimensions or diagrams were requested inside images. Registration
lines and typography are authored in HTML/CSS/SVG. Original generation outputs
remain outside the repository; these copies are the portable template sources.
