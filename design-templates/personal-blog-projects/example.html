Capture rule

Always store links.

  • Primary site URL and source URL when known.
  • Concrete page URLs for every page-level reference.
  • Concrete block URLs for every reusable pattern.
  • License status and reuse policy for each case.
Reuse rule

Distill, do not mirror.

  • Record structure, information architecture, and behavior.
  • Create original previews and component studies.
  • Avoid copying proprietary screenshots, assets, or source code.
  • Mark homepage-only captures honestly.
