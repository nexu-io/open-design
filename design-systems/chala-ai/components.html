What this fixture exercises

Every component below uses only var(--*) — no raw hex, no off-token type.

Opacity-only depth

No shadows, no gradients (except a single glow on the Landing CTA per --elev-raised). Lift comes from 1px hairlines at --border (8%) and --elev-ring (16%).

Inspect tokens →

Sharp interactive radii

--radius-sm: 2px on buttons / chips — chala's hard rule for interactive elements. Cards step to --radius-md: 8px for visual lift. Inputs use --radius-lg: 15px for softness on text fields.

Read the rule →

Uppercase mono chrome

Every label, eyebrow, timestamp, and stat caption is GeistMono at --text-xs with +0.18em tracking and text-transform: uppercase. The body face (Geist) is reserved for sentences.

Form components

Inputs read like instruments, not web forms.

Field labels are uppercase mono at --meta; the input itself sits on a 5% white fill with a 15px radius. Focus binds to --focus-ring — a 32% white halo, never the chromatic accent.
