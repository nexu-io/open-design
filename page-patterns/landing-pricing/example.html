Frequently asked

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade any time; we prorate the difference automatically.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans are refundable within 14 days. Monthly plans simply stop billing on cancellation.

How do team seats work?

You add or remove seats from the admin console; billing updates the next cycle.

Can I get an invoice?

Team plans include invoiced billing with net-30 terms. Pro plans can request invoicing via support.

Need something custom?

We work with larger orgs on procurement, SAML, and data residency.
