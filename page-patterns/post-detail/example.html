We have a working board list, a gallery grid, and a passable signup flow. The next bottleneck is the diagram surface that composes these page patterns into a multi-page site.

To get there, every pattern needs typed I/O — what kind of data it expects in, what kind of navigation it offers out. The first cut of that metadata landed last week.

What's next

A few items on the wishlist before the diagram surface unblocks:

Patterns should self-describe enough that the agent can decide where each new page belongs without prompting.

The eight seeds we just added cover roughly 80% of the page archetypes most sites need. The remaining 20% will follow once we have real usage data.
