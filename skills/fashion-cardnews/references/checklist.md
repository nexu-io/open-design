# Fashion Cardnews Checklist

Use this checklist before emitting the artifact.

## P0 — must pass

- [ ] Every card is 4:5 and designed to be readable as an independent social card.
- [ ] The carousel has explicit role separation: cover, index/checklist, look, detail, and outro.
- [ ] All images are either user-provided/safe or clearly labelled placeholders.
- [ ] The artifact contains no account cookies, tokens, upload logs, metrics, campaign IDs, or private performance data.
- [ ] No purchase CTA, price, discount, or link-push language appears on the cards.
- [ ] No unsupported claims such as “best”, “verified”, “sold out”, or “restocked” unless evidence is supplied in the brief.
- [ ] The cover headline is readable within one second.
- [ ] Text does not sit on low-contrast image zones without a deliberate readability treatment.

## P1 — quality bar

- [ ] Imagery or image slots carry most of the design weight; the deck does not feel like a text slide deck.
- [ ] Card numbering is integrated into the layout and does not look like a generic badge template.
- [ ] Body cards use one short editorial line, not paragraphs.
- [ ] Detail cards show useful fashion information: material, texture, silhouette, neckline, hem, accessories, or layering logic.
- [ ] The outro asks for saving/reference, not buying.
- [ ] Typography follows the active design system or uses a clear fallback hierarchy.
- [ ] Placeholder labels are honest and visually quiet.

## P2 — polish

- [ ] The rhythm alternates between wide hero crops, index/checklist, detail, and collage-style moments.
- [ ] The color system is restrained and derived from the fashion mood.
- [ ] Korean copy, when used, sounds like a feed-native fashion archive rather than an internal report.
- [ ] The final artifact includes `data-od-id` hooks on the stage, cards, major image slots, and headlines.
