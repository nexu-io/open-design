# Fashion Cardnews Asset Boundary

This skill is intentionally public-safe. It should hold reusable production
rules, layout systems, and placeholder templates — not live account content.

## Public-safe in this repository

- Layout recipes and role maps.
- Typography, spacing, color, and component guidance.
- Placeholder image slots and dummy examples.
- Generic generation scripts that do not depend on private accounts.
- QA checklists and export requirements.

## Keep outside this repository

- Final social post PNGs and videos.
- Real captions, drafts, post URLs, upload logs, and metrics.
- Product database exports, internal IDs, pricing, and experiment hypotheses.
- Scraped or brand-owned product photography unless cleared for public release.
- Browser sessions, cookies, tokens, and any automation credential.

## Recommended private workflow

1. Use this skill to generate or refine the reusable card system.
2. In a private content workspace, replace placeholders with approved campaign
   images and account-specific copy.
3. Run visual QA and policy checks in the private workspace.
4. Upload from the private workspace only.
5. Record post URLs, metrics, and iteration notes privately.
