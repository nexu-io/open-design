# Editorial Directions

Choose one direction before building. Do not mix direction systems.

## 1. Monocle Editorial

Restrained, international, knowledgeable, composed. Best default for business talks, founder narratives, and product strategy.

## 2. WIRED Tech

Data-forward, engineered, crisp, benchmark-friendly. Best for AI launches, technical product stories, research, and product demos.

## 3. Kinfolk Slow

Warm, quiet, literary, low-density. Best for private talks, essays, creator brands, and reflective narratives.

## 4. Domus Architectural

Spatial, geometric, portfolio-like. Best for design reviews, architecture, visual systems, and case studies.

## 5. Lab Reference

Methodical, academic, reproducible. Best for frameworks, field notes, research methods, and operating manuals.
